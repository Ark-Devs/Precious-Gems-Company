OUR HERITAGE

Eight Decades of Exquisite Artistry

Founded in 1942 in Colombo, Sri Lanka (formerly Ceylon, the world's legendary "Island of Gems"), Precious Gems Company has maintained a global reputation for importing, cutting, and exporting premium natural gemstones.

Every gem in our collection is ethically sourced from local alluvial mines in Ratnapura and meticulously hand-cut by master lapidarists. In our Colombo workshops, our veteran goldsmiths forge high-purity gold into custom creations that blend old-world Sri Lankan design with contemporary global sophistication.
